# Code of Conduct

## Contributor Covenant v3.0 Adoption

We adopt the Contributor Covenant, version 3.0, as our community standard to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward other community members.
- Offering and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on technical correctness, evidence, and what is best for the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private spaces, including unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission (doxxing).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the project maintainers via the project Telegram group:
